Transfer News

Stay up to date with the latest transfer news and rumors in the football world.

Reported 5 Oct 2026 | 3 min read

Super Eagles striker Taiwo Awoniyi has moved from Nottingham Forest to Coventry City on a permanent deal. The fee has been reported as £17 million.

The move is one of several by Nigeria internationals this summer. Frank Onyeka also joined Coventry City from Brentford, which means two Super Eagles players are now at the same club.

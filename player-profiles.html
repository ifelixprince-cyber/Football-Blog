Taiwo Awoniyi

A strong, hard-running striker who likes to battle defenders and hold the ball up. He creates space with his movement and works hard without the ball.

Awoniyi has been a regular in the Super Eagles since 2021, and he has scored some important goals. He is not a prolific scorer, but he is a team player who can create chances for others.

What it means

Nigeria are second on three points, three behind Guinea-Bissau, after a win and a defeat. Their two games against Tanzania in November come next. A win in both would put them on nine points and almost certainly through to the tournament. A win and a draw would also be enough, but a defeat in either game could leave them needing a result in the final match against Guinea-Bissau.

About Pitchside

Pitchside is a football blog about the Super Eagles and Nigerian football. We write about the matches, the transfers and the players that fans talk about, and we try to explain them clearly.

Why we started

Football talk moves fast, and not everything you read is true. Rumours spread as facts, and scores and tables get copied around with mistakes. Pitchside was started by a fan who wanted one place where the stories are easy to read, the facts are checked, and the opinions are labelled as opinions.

What you will find here

  • Match reports: what happened and why it mattered.
  • Transfer news: moves involving Nigerian players, each marked Confirmed, Reported or Rumour.
  • Player profiles: quick ID cards and longer stories on Super Eagles players.
  • Tables: the Super Eagles' group standings in qualifying.
  • Opinion: arguments about the team, such as why the midfield matters so much.

How we work

  • We link to the sources behind facts, so you can check them yourself.
  • We label transfer stories as Confirmed, Reported or Rumour, and we never present a rumour as fact.
  • We show a "last updated" date on pages that change, such as tables and transfers.
  • We write our own summaries instead of copying other sites.
  • We keep opinion separate from reporting, and say which is which.
  • We use only photos we took or have permission to use, and we credit them.

Spotted a mistake?

We get things wrong sometimes. If you find an error, tell us through the contact page and we will check it and fix it.
